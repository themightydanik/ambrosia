Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Ambrosia is a personal wellness and symptom-tracking application operated by an independent developer based in Ukraine (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Contact: themightydanik@gmail.com. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Ambrosia on the web or Android.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, Ambrosia may process account email and authentication identifiers; wellness journal entries, symptoms, dates, intensity, notes, check-ins, episodes and profile details; content you choose to submit to AI features; support correspondence; and purchase entitlement identifiers and subscription status. Wellness entries may reveal sensitive health-related information. Please avoid entering information you do not wish to store or process.

2. Local storage and optional synchronization

Ambrosia is designed to retain journal information locally on your device or browser. If you use an account and explicitly synchronize or import records, those records may also be stored in our Supabase-backed cloud database. Local device data and cloud data are distinct; signing in does not by itself mean that every previous guest record has been uploaded. Browser or device storage may be lost if you clear app data, uninstall the app or lose the device. We cannot guarantee recovery of records that were never synchronized or exported.

3. How we use information

  • Provide your journal, symptom history, check-ins, account features, synchronization and exports.
  • Authenticate users, protect account access, prevent abuse and resolve synchronization conflicts.
  • Process AI requests that you initiate, subject to the application's consent and access controls.
  • Verify purchases, subscription access and billing-related account entitlements.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot service failures and respond to support requests.

4. AI features and sensitive information

If you choose to use an AI feature, the relevant information you submit may be sent through our server-side service to a third-party AI provider (currently Groq) for processing. AI output is informational and is not a medical diagnosis. Do not submit information about other people without appropriate authority. AI requests are subject to in-app consent and technical limits; they are not required merely to read previously saved journal records. We do not intentionally include full journal contents in routine request/usage logs, although content necessary for a requested AI operation is transmitted to provide that operation.

5. Service providers

We use infrastructure and services that may include Supabase (authentication and database), Netlify (hosting and server functions), Groq (AI processing when invoked), Google (Google sign-in and Google Play services) and RevenueCat (subscription entitlement processing). Each provider processes relevant information under its own terms and privacy documentation. We do not sell your personal health journal information to advertisers.

6. Subscriptions and payment information

Android subscriptions, when available, are purchased through Google Play and checked through RevenueCat and our server. We receive purchase identifiers, entitlement state and relevant transaction metadata, not your full payment-card details. Actual pricing, eligibility for free trials, billing terms and renewal dates are shown by Google Play before purchase. Your subscription history may be retained where required for financial, security, dispute or legal obligations.

7. Legal grounds and international processing

Where applicable, we process information to perform the service you request, based on your consent for optional sensitive-data/AI processing, to protect the service against misuse, and to meet legal obligations. Because our service providers may operate outside Ukraine or your country of residence, data may be processed internationally, subject to applicable safeguards and provider arrangements. You may withdraw optional consent through available controls or by contacting us; this does not necessarily undo processing already lawfully completed.

8. Retention, deletion and export

You can use the app's export and account-deletion functions, where available, or contact themightydanik@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion. Account deletion is designed to remove account-associated cloud records; locally stored copies, downloaded exports, device backups, and information held independently by payment providers may require separate action. Limited security, aggregated usage or transaction records may remain where reasonably necessary or legally required. We do not promise a fixed retention period for all data categories; retention depends on the record type, active account status and applicable obligations.

9. Security

We use authenticated account access, database access controls and server-side verification for protected operations. No system or transmission is perfectly secure. Protect your login credentials and any exported journal files, which may contain sensitive information.

10. Children

Ambrosia is not intended for children to use independently. Do not create an account or provide personal health information if you are below the age at which you may legally consent to such processing in your location without the required parent or guardian authorization. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted information without appropriate authorization.

11. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or erase your personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority. To exercise rights, email us; we may need to verify that you control the relevant account.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the service or legal requirements. The effective date above will change when material updates are published. Questions and privacy requests: themightydanik@gmail.com.
